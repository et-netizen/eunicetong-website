Interactive 3D

Iris the Chameleon

A single-file 3D chameleon that you can orbit, zoom and tap. Iris idles, sleeps, and climbs a tree. The whole page is one HTML file built with three.js.

  • Built step by step with Claude, from a plain brief to a finished page.
  • Procedural scale texture on the skin, with no external images.
  • Hand-built movement: climbing paths along the trunk, a tail that rolls and unrolls, and tap reactions.
  • Lighting, camera and layout tuned for phones as well as desktops.
